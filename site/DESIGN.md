# Design direction

The user's final instruction: follow leerob.com closely; remove the guestbook and the centered shud.in-like design.

## Decisions

- 600px reading column on the left; 35% width sticky illustration on the right.
- Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia serif stack; 17px body with 1.6 line-height.
- 42.4px name, 23.2px section headings, small sans-serif Bio controls and dates.
- White background, dark gray ink, muted underlined links, square list bullets.
- At phone widths, text becomes one column and the illustration follows the content.
- A single biography with no length toggle, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support.

Taste Skill's initial direction was “The quiet instrument,” seed 373816603. The user rejected the resulting narrow centered design. Their subsequent explicit reference overrides that sheet, decorative motion requirements, no-hairline rules, and picture-count requirements. The generated mockups from the earlier direction are superseded.

## Artwork

Built-in image generation produced an original tall editorial landscape. Final asset: `public/images/landscape.webp`.

Prompt: Original tall 2:3 editorial screen-print/gouache illustration on textured ivory paper; imaginary colorful modern city, sky-blue sky and cream clouds, pale blue river, green hills, gold grasses, red poppies and pink flowers; cheerful travel-poster atmosphere; no named city, recognizable landmark, letters, logos, UI, or existing artwork reproduction.

The scene is fictional, not a claim about the portfolio owner's home or life. Optimized to a 1000px-wide WebP.
